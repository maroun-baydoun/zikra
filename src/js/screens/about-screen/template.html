About Zikra

Putting the pieces back together

What is Zikra?

Zikra, Arabic for memory, is a puzzle game built from photographs taken over the years.

How to play

Choose a photo and rebuild it by swapping two pieces at a time. Tap one piece to select it, then tap another piece to exchange their positions. Your time starts after the initial shuffle and stops when the photo is complete.

Your data

Your settings and best times are saved locally in your browser.
